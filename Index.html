THE TOURNAMENT

A Tradition Begins

Founded during the summer of 2025, the Wildcat Cup brings together eighteen Marin Catholic High School alumni for an annual two-day, two-man scramble.

The tournament is contested over 36 holes across two of Marin County's premier golf courses— Indian Valley Golf Club and Peacock Gap Golf Club.

More than just a tournament, the Wildcat Cup is about lifelong friendships, competition, and creating traditions that continue every summer.
