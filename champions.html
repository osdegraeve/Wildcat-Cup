THE TROPHY

The Wildcat Cup

Awarded annually to the champions of the Wildcat Cup, the trophy represents competition, sportsmanship, and lifelong friendships formed at Marin Catholic High School.

Each summer, a new team earns the right to have their names engraved into tournament history.
